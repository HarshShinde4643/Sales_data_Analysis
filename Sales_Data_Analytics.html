Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)¶

Q1. What is the age distribution of customers?¶

In [ ]:
plt.figure(figsize=(8, 5))
sns.histplot(customer['age'], bins=15)
plt.title('Customer Age Distribution',size=20)
plt.xlabel('Age')
plt.ylabel('Number of customers')
plt.show()

Analysis:¶

  • The histogram shows the distribution of customers across different ages, from approximately 18 to 70 years.

Conclusion:¶

  • The business serves customers across a broad range of ages.
  • The average customer age is approximately 44 years.

Q2. What is the distribution of sales revenue?¶

In [ ]:
plt.figure(figsize=(8, 5))
plt.hist(sales["revenue"], bins=10)
plt.title("Distribution of Sales Revenue")
plt.xlabel("Revenue")
plt.ylabel("Frequency")
plt.show()

Analysis:¶

  • Most transactions generate revenue in the lower-to-middle ranges, particularly around 10–30 units of revenue.

Conclusion:¶

  • Most sales transactions generate relatively modest revenue, while high-revenue transactions are less frequent.
  • The business should investigate which products, quantities, or discounts contribute to higher-revenue transactions

Q3.Which product category contains the highest number of products?¶

In [ ]:
data = product['category'].value_counts()

data.plot(kind='bar',
           color='blue', 
           figsize=(7, 4))
plt.title('Number of Products by Category')
plt.ylabel('Number of products')
plt.grid(alpha=1)
plt.show()

Analysis:¶

  • Praline has the highest number of products, with approximately 53 products.
  • White chocolate has approximately 48 products.
  • Dark chocolate has approximately 42 products.
  • Truffle has approximately 31 products.

Conclusion:¶

  • Praline has the largest product assortment, while Milk chocolate has the smallest.
  • The business can review product availability and category representation to identify gaps in its catalog.

Q4. Which product category contributes the highest share of revenue?¶

In [ ]:
category_revenue = sales.merge(product[['product_id', 'category']],
                               on='product_id').groupby('category')['revenue'].sum()

category_revenue.plot(
	kind='pie',
	autopct='%1.1f%%',
	figsize=(7, 7),
	ylabel='',
	title='Revenue Share by Product Category'
)
plt.show()

Analysis:¶

  • Praline contributes the highest share of total revenue, accounting for approximately 27.4%.
  • White chocolate contributes approximately 24.2% of total revenue.
  • Dark chocolate accounts for approximately 20.7%.
  • Truffle contributes approximately 15.0%.
  • Milk chocolate contributes the lowest share, at approximately 12.7%.
  • Praline and White chocolate together account for approximately 51.6% of total revenue

Conclusion:¶

  • Praline is the leading revenue-generating category, followed by White chocolate.
  • These categories represent important contributors to overall sales revenue.

Q5. What is the relationship between unit price and profit?¶

In [ ]:
plt.scatter(sales["unit_price"], 
            sales["profit"],
            alpha=0.5,
            marker='o',
            edgecolors='black',
            color='red')
plt.title("Unit Price vs Profit")
plt.xlabel("Unit Price")
plt.ylabel("Profit")
plt.show()

Analysis:¶

  • The scatter plot shows a generally positive relationship between unit price and profit.
  • Transactions involving higher unit prices can generate higher profits.

Conclusion:¶

  • Unit price appears to be positively associated with profit in this dataset.
  • The business should analyze unit price alongside quantity, discounts, and cost to understand the factors driving profitability.

Overall Project Conclusion:-¶

1.Customer demographics: Customers span a broad age range, with an average age of approximately 44 years and a nearly balanced gender distribution.

2.Revenue distribution: Most transactions generate lower-to-middle revenue, while high-revenue transactions are less frequent.

3.Product assortment: Praline has the largest number of products in the catalog, while Milk chocolate has the smallest.

4.Revenue contribution: Praline generates the highest revenue share at approximately 27.4%, followed by White chocolate at 24.2%.

5.Pricing and profitability: Unit price shows a positive relationship with profit, although other factors also affect profitability.

6.Revenue and profit variability: Both measures contain high-value outliers that could be investigated to understand exceptional transactions.

Final conclusion:¶

  • The analysis provides a useful overview of customer characteristics, product assortment, revenue contribution, and profit patterns.
